Progress Keeper keeps local copies of your Geometry Dash progress close at hand.

Drag the floating save button to a comfortable position. Press it to make a backup immediately, or enter an interval and choose S, M or H to schedule recurring backups. Use Stop whenever you want to disable the schedule.

The button stays out of the way while a level is running. If a scheduled time is reached during gameplay, saving continues after you return to a menu. The newest 20 backups are retained automatically.
